ARTICLE 3 — PHASE STRUCTURE
================================================================================

3.1 The project starts at Trial. It may later advance to Pilot, Launch and Scale. A project may
    start at any phase; in this Agreement the Parties have chosen to start at Trial.

3.2 PHASE 1 — EVALUATION PERIOD (maps to NuBred TRIAL — Experimental)

    Name as used in this Agreement: "Evaluation Period" / "VCU Phase".
    Objective: Screen the Licensed Varieties (including NPV-Yellow-21) with few plants per
    Selection to identify commercial potential. Conduct VCU evaluation. No commercial activity.
    Duration: 4 growing seasons from the Release Date (1 September 2024).
    Start trigger: Release Date notified by Licensor (1 September 2024).
    Location: Trial sites at Lleida (Catalonia, Spain) and Vale do Tejo (Portugal).
    Plant count: 120 plants in total for Trial (40 plants per genotype).
    Hectares: 0.12 ha.
    Gate criteria to advance from Trial to Pilot:
      (a) all Observational Protocol observations for Trial completed per Annex 5;
      (b) Variety Manager (Licensor) Promote / Repeat / Discard decision per genotype;
      (c) annual VCU report accepted by Licensor.
    Capitolato defined: not applicable at Trial (null for this phase).

    Genotype decisions recorded at the Trial/Pilot gate meeting of 12 October 2026:
      - Smeralda: PROMOTE to Pilot. Notes: VCU positive; Brix and firmness above threshold.
      - Dorì Gold: PROMOTE to Pilot. Notes: colour and shelf life acceptable.
      - NPV-Yellow-21: REPEAT Trial for one additional growing season. Notes: insufficient
        fruit-set data; PSA screening incomplete. Decision date: 12 October 2026.

3.3 PHASE 2 — SEMI-COMMERCIAL VALIDATION (maps to NuBred PILOT — Experimental)

    Name as used in this Agreement: "Semi-Commercial Validation" / "Pilot Phase".
    Objective: Validate Smeralda and Dorì Gold at semi-commercial scale (approximately 0.5–1 ha)
    and produce a first production forecast. No commercial sale of fruit. Brand royalty does
    not apply. Observational Protocol continues.
    Duration: 2 growing seasons, starting the season after Trial gate confirmation.
    Start trigger: written VM confirmation of Trial gate passage (target 1 March 2027).
    Location: Pilot orchards in Lleida (Spain) and Ribatejo (Portugal).
    Plant count: 1,600 plants (800 Smeralda, 800 Dorì Gold).
    Hectares: 0.80 ha.
    Gate criteria to advance from Pilot to Launch:
      (a) all Pilot protocol observations completed;
      (b) production forecast validated by both Parties;
      (c) Capitolato (Quality Specification) defined and signed as Annex 7 — HARD GATE:
          Launch is blocked until the Capitolato is defined;
      (d) Variety Manager advance confirmation.
    Capitolato defined: YES — Annex 7 was signed on 20 January 2028.
    Genotype decisions at Pilot close (15 February 2028):
      - Smeralda: PROMOTE to Launch. Notes: Capitolato met in 2027 harvest sample.
      - Dorì Gold: PROMOTE to Launch. Notes: pack-out 72%.
      Decision date: 15 February 2028.

3.4 PHASE 3 — COMMERCIAL INTRODUCTION (maps to NuBred LAUNCH — Commercial)

    Name as used in this Agreement: "Commercial Introduction" / "Market Introduction".
    Objective: First commercial season. PBR and Brand royalties become active. Licensee
    plants according to Year-1 Production Plan. Fruit supplied only to Authorised Distributors.
    Duration: 1 commercial season (2028/2029), then eligible for Scale.
    Start trigger: Pilot gate confirmation AND Capitolato in force (target 1 March 2028).
    Location: Production Territory (Spain, Portugal, Balearic Islands).
    Plant count: 10,000 plants in Launch year (Year 1 Minimum Quantity).
    Hectares: 5.0 ha.
    Gate criteria to advance from Launch to Scale:
      (a) production forecast validated for the Launch season;
      (b) royalty reporting complete (commercialisation report + royalty payment);
      (c) Variety Manager advance confirmation.
    Capitolato defined: not a Launch-specific gate (already defined at Pilot).
    Commercial Protocol (Annex 6) applies from Launch. Observational Protocol does not continue.

3.5 PHASE 4 — FULL COMMERCIAL EXPANSION (maps to NuBred SCALE — Commercial)

    Name as used in this Agreement: "Full Commercial Expansion" / "Market Phase".
    Objective: Expansion per Production Plan. Full royalty flows. Delta EBITDA calculation
    active under the Advanced Commercial Protocol.
    Duration: until expiry of CPVO PBR in EU territory (expected 31 December 2056), subject
    to renewal under Article 16.
    Start trigger: Launch gate confirmation (target 1 March 2029).
    Location: Production Territory, with commercialisation in Commercialisation Territory.
    Plant count: 50,000 plants from Year 3 onwards (steady-state Minimum Quantity).
    Hectares: 25.0 ha at steady state.
    Gate criteria: ongoing production forecast cycle; royalty reporting each season.

ARTICLE 7 — MINIMUM QUANTITIES
================================================================================

7.1 Minimum quantities per season (Finished Plants planted, unit: plants):
    Year 1 (Launch 2028/29): 10,000 plants.
    Year 2 (Scale 2029/30): 25,000 plants.
    Year 3 and each subsequent season: 50,000 plants.

7.2 Tolerance: minus fifteen percent (−15%) below the applicable Minimum Quantity is permitted
    without triggering Step 1 enforcement.

7.3 Recovery years: two (2) consecutive seasons to recover before enforcement escalates from
    Step 1 to Step 2.

7.4 Enforcement cascade:
    Step 1: lump-sum indemnity calculated per the formula in Annex 6 (shortfall plants × €2.50).
    Step 2 (if repeated after recovery window): revocation of exclusivity for commercialisation
    in the Commercialisation Territory.
    Step 3 (if persisted a further season): termination under Article 17.2(b).

7.5 Maximum Quantities: 80,000 plants per season without written authorisation. Exceeding
    Maximum Quantities without authorisation is a material breach.

     Soluble solids (Brix): ≥ 15.5 °Brix at harvest (Quantitative Absolute).

     No quarantine pathogen detected (Boolean eliminatory — PSA / Pseudomonas syringae pv.
     actinidiae: zero tolerance).

ARTICLE 13 — OBSERVATIONAL PROTOCOL (TRIAL AND PILOT)
================================================================================

13.1 Protocol name: "VCU Evaluation Protocol — Kiwifruit (Observational)".
     Protocol type: OBSERVATIONAL.
     Linked phase: TRIAL (also governs PILOT with the same parameter set; extract as
     OBSERVATIONAL linked to TRIAL / PILOT as applicable — two observational entries may
     be recorded, one per phase, using this Annex 5 source).
     Source document: this Agreement — Annex 5 "VCU Evaluation Protocol — Kiwifruit".
     Objective: Produce a trial report and a Promote / Repeat / Discard decision per genotype.

13.2 Parameters (Annex 5). Extract each of the following.

     (1) Plant vigour
         Original name: Tree vigour / vigour score
         NuBred standard name: Plant Vigour
         Family F1 — Plant Characteristics. Level: SPECIES_STANDARD.
         Input type: SCALE. Scale min 1, scale max 9, polarity HIGHER_BETTER.
         How to measure: Visual assessment on 30 plants per plot per UPOV TG/13/10 descriptor 16.
         Frequency type: CALENDAR_FIXED. Frequency value: 3× per season (fruit set, 60 days
         pre-harvest, harvest). Window: BBCH 71–89. Sample size: 30 plants per genotype.
         Threshold: UPOV Qualitative: class ≥ 5 for vigour. Eliminatory level: L2.

     (2) BBCH phenology
         Name: BBCH stage
         Family F1. Level: UNIVERSAL. Input type: OPTION (BBCH stages).
         How to measure: Record first flowering, full bloom, fruit set, first harvest, last harvest.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — per BBCH stage transition.
         Window: BBCH 60–89. Sample: 10 plants per genotype.
         Threshold: none. Eliminatory level: null.
         Related dates: first flowering DATE; first harvest DATE; last harvest DATE
         (extract flowering and harvest dates as DATE parameters if listed separately:
         "Date of first flowering", "Date of first harvest", "Date of last harvest",
         input_type DATE, family F1, EVENT_BASED, per occurrence).

     (3) Soluble solids content
         Original name: Soluble solids content
         NuBred standard name: Brix
         Family F2 — Fruit Quality. Level: SPECIES_STANDARD.
         Input type: NUMBER. Unit: °Brix.
         How to measure: Refractometer on juice extracted from min 5 fruits.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest event.
         Window: Within 7 days of commercial harvest. Sample: 30 fruit per plot.
         Threshold: Quantitative Absolute: ≥15.5°Brix (Pilot/Launch Capitolato);
         Trial screening threshold ≥14.0°Brix. Eliminatory level: L2.

     (4) Firmness
         Name: Flesh firmness
         Family F2. Level: SPECIES_STANDARD.
         Input type: NUMBER. Unit: kg/0.5cm².
         How to measure: Penetrometer with 0.5cm² tip — min 10 fruits.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest event.
         Sample: 30 fruit per plot.
         Threshold: Quantitative Absolute: ≥6.0 kg/0.5cm² at packing. Eliminatory: L2.

     (5) Fruit weight
         Name: Average fruit weight
         Family F2. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: g.
         How to measure: Mean of 50 fruits per plot, calibrated scale.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest event. Sample: min 50 fruits per sample.
         Threshold: Quantitative Absolute: 90–130 g. Eliminatory: L3 (project-custom band).

     (6) Organoleptic score
         Name: Taste / organoleptic
         Family F2. Input type: SCALE. Scale min 1, scale max 5, polarity HIGHER_BETTER.
         How to measure: Trained panel, 8 tasters, blind coded samples.
         Frequency: PHASE_GATE — required before Promote from Pilot.
         Sample: 20 fruit per genotype.
         Threshold: UPOV Qualitative: mean ≥ 3.5. Eliminatory: L3.

     (7) Yield
         Original name: Production per plant
         NuBred standard name: Production
         Family F3. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: kg/plant (also record kg/ha).
         How to measure: All fruit harvested from 10 marked plants per genotype, weighed.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest event.
         Threshold: Quantitative Relative: ≥15% above Benchmark Hayward at same site
         from Pilot year 2. Eliminatory: L3.

     (8) Calibre distribution
         Name: Calibre distribution
         Family F3. Input type: DISTRIBUTION (histogram % per size class summing to 100%).
         Classes: <80g / 80–99g / 100–119g / 120–139g / ≥140g.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest event. Sample: min 50 fruits.
         Threshold: none. Eliminatory: null.

     (9) Pack-out percentage
         Name: Pack-out %
         Family F3. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: %.
         Frequency: EVENT_BASED — Per harvest. Threshold: ≥70% Class I from Pilot.
         Eliminatory: L3.

     (10) PSA / bacterial canker
          Original name: Pseudomonas syringae pv. actinidiae (Psa)
          Family F4 — Phytosanitary. Level: UNIVERSAL.
          Input type: BOOLEAN.
          How to measure: Visual plus laboratory PCR on suspect tissue. Zero tolerance.
          Frequency: CALENDAR_FIXED — Weekly during active season; also ON_DEMAND for incidents.
          Threshold: Boolean eliminatory: No quarantine pathogen detected.
          Eliminatory level: L1 (universal — no VM override; PSA for yellow kiwi).

     (11) Botrytis / storage rot
          Name: Botrytis incidence
          Family F4. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: %.
          Frequency: EVENT_BASED — at packing and after storage.
          Threshold: Quantitative Absolute: ≤5% after 8 weeks. Eliminatory: L2.

     (12) Cold storage evaluation
          Original name: Cold storage evaluation
          NuBred standard name: Post-Harvest Evaluation
          Family F5. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: % damaged/rotten and % weight loss.
          How to measure: At 4, 8, 12, 16 weeks in cold storage (0–1°C).
          Frequency: CALENDAR_FIXED — 4-week interval in store.
          Window: At 4, 8, 12, 16 weeks in cold storage (0–1°C).
          Sample: 50 fruit per genotype per pull.
          Threshold: Quantitative Absolute: ≤8% damaged at 12 weeks. Eliminatory: L2.

     (13) Shelf life
          Name: Shelf life (days at 20°C after store)
          Family F5. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: days.
          Frequency: EVENT_BASED — per storage pull. Threshold: ≥10 days. Eliminatory: L3.

     (14) Temperature
          Name: Mean daily temperature
          Family F6 — Climate. Input type: NUMBER. Unit: °C.
          How to measure: Daily — continuous weather station logging.
          Frequency: CALENDAR_FIXED. Frequency value: Daily — continuous weather station logging.
          Threshold: none (contextual, no threshold). Eliminatory: null.

     (15) Irrigation log
          Original name: Irrigation log
          Family F8 — Agronomic Operations. Input type: TEXT (volume also NUMBER, unit mm).
          Frequency: CALENDAR_FIXED — weekly operational log.
          How to measure: Water applied (mm) and method (drip/microjet) recorded by Field Observer.
          Threshold: none. Eliminatory: null.

     (16) Fertilization record
