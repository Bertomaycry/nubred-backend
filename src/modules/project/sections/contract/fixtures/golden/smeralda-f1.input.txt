ARTICLE 5 — TERM, TERMINATION, PENALTIES, LAW

Term: from 15 March 2024 until expiry of CPVO PBR for Smeralda (expected 31 December 2056).
Automatic renewal if PBR is extended unless 180 days' written notice of non-renewal.
Either Party may elect not to renew.

EXPRESS termination (no notice, art. 1456 Italian Civil Code equivalent): non-payment of
royalties for more than 30 days; failure to meet Minimum Quantities for 2 consecutive
seasons after recovery; unauthorised propagation; sale of fruit outside Commercialisation
Territory; failure to notify an EDV within 15 days.

ORDINARY termination: either Party for convenience with 180 days' notice after the
Evaluation Period; Licensee if Licensor fails to maintain PBR and does not cure within
90 days.

Penalties: plants from unauthorised source €5.00 per plant, LICENSEE to LICENSOR;
false census 10× declared royalties, LICENSEE to LICENSOR.

Surviving obligations: Confidentiality indefinitely; EDV notification indefinitely;
plant destruction within 30 days post-termination; IP non-challenge 5 years
post-termination; breeding limitation 20 years for VCU data.

Governing law: Italian law. Forum: Court of Bologna. Licensor may unilaterally bring
proceedings before any other competent court in the Production Territory.
Arbitration: false.

Capitolato (Annex 7, signed 20 January 2028): Brix ≥ 15.5 °Brix; firmness ≥ 6.0 kg/0.5cm²;
zero tolerance for PSA (Pseudomonas syringae pv. actinidiae).
